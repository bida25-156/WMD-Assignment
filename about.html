About Sonic Lab

Our Mission

To equip Botswana's musicians with quality instruments — drums, keyboards, lead guitars, and bass guitars — without the hassle of limited local options or unreliable overseas shipping.

Who We Are

Founder: Thero Bagwasi

Store name: Sonic Lab

Founded: 2026 Location: Botswana (online only) Sonic Lab was born from a simple need: getting the right gear without endless waiting or broken promises. As a musician myself, I (Thero) started this store so local players could find reliable instruments — from thundering kick drums to screaming lead guitars — all from one Botswana-based online shop.

What We Carry

Drums – shell packs, cymbals, hardware Keyboards – digital pianos, synths, MIDI controllers Lead guitars – electric guitars built for solos and stage work Bass guitars – 4-string, 5-string, and practice amps Why Shop at Sonic Lab? Botswana-only online store – No surprise cross-border fees or month-long waits. Hand-picked stock – Every instrument is chosen for durability, playability, and value. Straight talk – No marketing fluff. If a guitar neck feels sticky or a drum lug is stiff, you’ll know before you buy. Support from a player – I’m Thero, not a call center. Need setup advice or a second opinion? You’ll talk to me. Our Promise Secure checkout with local delivery options across Botswana Clear photos & specs for every listing Honest condition notes (all instruments are new unless stated otherwise) Let’s Build the Sound Sonic Lab is just getting started, but the goal is simple: help you find the right instrument so you can focus on playing, not searching.
